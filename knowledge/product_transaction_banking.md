# Product One-Pager: Transaction Banking

Northlake Bank (fictional), synthetic. Product ID P06. Revenue type: recurring. Capital intensity: low.

## What it is
Cash management (operating accounts, payments, collections, liquidity sweeps) and trade finance (letters of credit, guarantees, supply-chain finance).

## Revenue drivers
- Deposit income: the margin earned on client operating balances, about 45% of Transaction Banking revenue.
- Transaction and service fees on payments, collections, account services and trade instruments.
- Balances move with client liquidity and the rate environment; fees move with payment volumes.
- Revenue accrues every month and is the most stable line in the business.

## Capital intensity
Low. Cash management uses almost no capital; trade finance uses some, on short tenors.

## Typical clients
Every segment, with the deepest use among Large Corporate and Financial Institutions clients.

## Cross-sell pairings
- Lending: winning the operating account of a borrower is the single most effective way to lift a lending-only client's RAROC.
- Rates & FX Hedging: FX on cross-border payment flows.

## What to watch
Operating balances, fee volumes, client attrition, and the monthly reconciliation of fee and deposit-income bookings to the general ledger. Transaction Banking books many small fee entries, so a missed or duplicated batch shows up as a reconciliation break between the GL control total and booked revenue.
