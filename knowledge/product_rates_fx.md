# Product One-Pager: Rates & FX Hedging

Northlake Bank (fictional), synthetic. Product ID P05. Revenue type: recurring. Capital intensity: medium.

## What it is
Interest-rate swaps, caps and FX forwards and options sold to corporate and institutional clients to hedge loan interest, bond issuance and foreign-currency revenue or costs.

## Revenue drivers
- Sales credit (the margin built into each hedge) earned when the trade is executed, plus ongoing flow from clients that hedge regularly.
- Flow is driven by client activity: new loans and bonds create hedging needs, and exporters and importers hedge FX monthly.
- Rate and currency volatility increases hedging demand.

## Capital intensity
Medium. Derivatives create counterparty credit exposure that attracts capital, mostly on longer-dated swaps with lower-rated clients.

## Typical clients
Financial Institutions (the heaviest users), Large Corporate exporters and importers, and borrowers with floating-rate debt.

## Cross-sell pairings
- Lending: a swap on every floating-rate term loan is the most common pairing and a quick win for lending-only clients.
- Debt Capital Markets: fixed-to-floating and cross-currency swaps on new bonds.
- Transaction Banking: FX conversion on international payment flows.

## What to watch
Share of borrowers that hedge, sales credit per trade, and counterparty exposure on long-dated trades.
