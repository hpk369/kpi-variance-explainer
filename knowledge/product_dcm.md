# Product One-Pager: Debt Capital Markets

Northlake Bank (fictional), synthetic. Product ID P03. Revenue type: event. Capital intensity: low.

## What it is
Arranging and underwriting investment-grade and high-yield bonds, private placements, and syndicated loans for corporates, financial institutions and sponsors.

## Revenue drivers
- Underwriting and arrangement fees earned when a deal prices, as a percentage of the amount raised.
- Issuance volume follows the market window: credit spreads, rate volatility and blackout periods around earnings decide when clients come to market.
- Financial-institution clients issue regularly (senior and covered bonds), which makes their DCM revenue steadier than corporate DCM.
- Refinancing a large loan in the bond market earns a DCM fee but can reduce Lending revenue afterwards.

## Capital intensity
Low, apart from short underwriting exposure between pricing and settlement.

## Typical clients
Financial Institutions and Large Corporate issuers; Sponsors for leveraged-loan refinancing.

## Cross-sell pairings
- Rates & FX Hedging: swapping fixed-rate bond proceeds to floating, or hedging cross-currency issuance.
- Lending: bridge facilities ahead of a bond, and backstop revolvers.
- M&A Advisory: acquisition financing takeouts.

## What to watch
Issuance windows, the forward calendar of mandated deals, league-table share among Canadian issuers, and fee rates on repeat issuers.
