# Product One-Pager: Equity Capital Markets

Northlake Bank (fictional), synthetic. Product ID P04. Revenue type: event. Capital intensity: low.

## What it is
Initial public offerings, follow-on offerings, block trades and convertible bonds, mainly for Large Corporate and sponsor-backed companies.

## Revenue drivers
- Gross spread (underwriting fee) earned when the offering prices; Northlake's share depends on its role in the syndicate (bookrunner or co-manager).
- Volumes are highly sensitive to equity-market levels and volatility. IPO windows can open and close within weeks.
- Sponsor exits through IPOs link ECM revenue to the private-equity cycle.

## Capital intensity
Low, except for bought deals and blocks, where the bank holds shares for a short time.

## Typical clients
Large Corporate issuers raising growth or acquisition capital; Sponsors listing portfolio companies; Financial Institutions raising regulatory capital.

## Cross-sell pairings
- M&A Advisory: dual-track sale or IPO processes, and equity-funded acquisitions.
- Debt Capital Markets: convertibles and post-IPO debt refinancing.
- Transaction Banking: treasury services for newly listed companies.

## What to watch
Market volatility, the backlog of filed and mandated offerings, bookrunner share, and the timing risk of deals that depend on a market window.
