# Product One-Pager: M&A Advisory

Northlake Bank (fictional), synthetic. Product ID P02. Revenue type: event. Capital intensity: low.

## What it is
Sell-side and buy-side advice on mergers, acquisitions, divestitures and sponsor exits, plus fairness opinions. Mandates run from a few months to more than a year.

## Revenue drivers
- Success fees paid at closing, typically a percentage of transaction value with a minimum fee. Almost all M&A Advisory revenue is a success fee.
- Retainers and announcement fees are small and are netted into the success fee.
- Revenue is recognised only on the day the transaction closes. M&A Advisory is therefore the lumpiest line in CIB: one or two large mandates moving by a few weeks can swing a month or a quarter by several million dollars.
- Close timing depends on regulatory approvals, financing, shareholder votes and buyer due diligence, most of which the bank does not control.

## Capital intensity
Low. Advisory uses almost no balance sheet, so a fee lifts client RAROC sharply. One mandate can take a lending-only client above the hurdle for a full year.

## Typical clients
Large Corporate clients for strategic acquisitions and divestitures; Sponsors for portfolio-company exits; selected Mid-Market owners for sale processes.

## Cross-sell pairings
- Acquisition financing (Lending) and bond or loan takeout (Debt Capital Markets) for the buyer.
- Equity Capital Markets for a carve-out IPO or an equity-funded acquisition.
- Rates & FX Hedging for deal-contingent hedges on cross-border transactions.

## What to watch
Mandates in Execution and their expected close dates, slipped deals and the reason for each slip, weighted pipeline by stage, and concentration: how much of a quarter's plan depends on a handful of mandates. When a mandate slips, commentary reports a timing variance and the new expected close date.
