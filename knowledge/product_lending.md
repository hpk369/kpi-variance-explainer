# Product One-Pager: Lending

Northlake Bank (fictional), synthetic. Product ID P01. Revenue type: recurring. Capital intensity: high.

## What it is
Committed revolving credit facilities, term loans and acquisition financing for corporate, mid-market, sponsor-backed and financial-institution clients. Lending is usually the first product in a new relationship and the anchor of most coverage plans.

## Revenue drivers
- Net interest income on drawn balances, driven by average drawn balance × credit spread. About 90% of Lending revenue is net interest income.
- Arrangement and upfront fees when a facility is signed, extended or amended.
- Commitment (standby) fees on undrawn amounts.
- Utilisation moves with the cycle: clients draw more in a slowdown and repay after a capital-markets refinancing, so a DCM bond deal can reduce Lending revenue the following month.

## Capital intensity
High. Drawn and committed amounts attract credit-risk capital, and expected loss is charged against revenue in RAROC. A lending-only relationship rarely clears the 15% hurdle on its own; it relies on fee products to lift returns.

## Typical clients
Every segment. Mid-Market clients are most often lending-only, which is why they dominate the list of clients below the hurdle.

## Cross-sell pairings
- Transaction Banking: operating accounts and cash management for the borrower, low capital, sticky deposits.
- Rates & FX Hedging: interest-rate swaps to hedge a floating-rate loan, FX forwards for exporters.
- Debt Capital Markets: refinancing larger facilities in the bond or syndicated-loan market.

## What to watch
Average drawn balances, spread compression on renewals, expected loss on lower-rated names, and the RAROC of lending-only clients against the hurdle.
