# Management Commentary: July 2026

Northlake Bank (fictional), synthetic. Monthly performance commentary for the CIB Executive Committee, prepared by CIB Business Management. Figures as reported at month-end close; all amounts in CAD.

## Headline
CIB revenue was $66.7M in July, $1.8M (2.7%) below plan. The miss is timing in M&A Advisory, partly offset by an unplanned financial-institutions bond deal. Recurring revenue was in line.

## Revenue vs plan
- **M&A Advisory, −$2.7M vs plan.** A sponsor portfolio-company exit planned for July did not close in the month. The buyer's financing is committed and the deal is expected to close in August, so we are treating this as timing, not a loss.
- **Debt Capital Markets, +$1.4M vs plan.** A Financial Institutions client brought forward a senior bond that was not in the plan.
- **Lending, −$0.4M vs plan.** Spread compression on two Large Corporate renewals and lower average drawn balances.
- Rates & FX Hedging and Transaction Banking were on plan.
- Industrials revenue was $12.6M against a $12.9M plan.

## Pipeline and coverage
- The August plan depends heavily on two Industrials M&A sell-side mandates in Execution: $4.5M and $3.0M of expected fees, both targeting late-August closings. Together they are $7.5M, more than a third of Industrials' August plan. Coverage East 1 is tracking both weekly.
- Coverage East 2's win rate fell to 30% in Q2 from 44% in Q4 2025. The team took on new Mid-Market relationships this year and is running above its client-per-banker target. We will bring a capacity proposal in September.

## Controls
- The June month-end reconciliation shows Transaction Banking's general ledger control total $1.2M above booked revenue. A positive gap normally means a fee batch was not booked. Product Control has opened a break under the Data-Quality and Reconciliation Break SOP; it is not yet resolved.

## Actions
1. Coverage East 1 to confirm closing timetables for the two Industrials mandates by August 15 (owner: Head of Coverage East).
2. Product Control to identify the missing Transaction Banking booking and post the correction (owner: CIB Finance).
3. Business Management to draft the Coverage East 2 capacity proposal (owner: CIB COO).
