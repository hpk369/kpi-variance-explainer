# Management Commentary: September 2026 and Q3 2026

Northlake Bank (fictional), synthetic. Quarter-end performance commentary for the Executive Committee, prepared by Business Management. Figures as reported at quarter-end close; all amounts in CAD. Q3 means calendar Q3 (July to September).

## Headline
Q3 revenue was $207.3M, $8.7M (4.0%) below plan. Industrials accounts for the whole miss: two M&A mandates that slipped out of August explain $7.5M, about 85% of the Industrials gap. The rest of the franchise finished the quarter broadly on plan. September alone was $60.7M, $0.9M below plan.

## Q3 revenue vs plan
- **Industrials, −$8.8M vs plan** ($38.0M against $46.8M). M&A Advisory accounts for $7.5M (85%): the two sell-side mandates ($4.5M and $3.0M) are still in Execution and targeting mid-November closings. Lending to Industrials clients was $1.3M short (15%), on lower drawn balances.
- **Other industries, broadly on plan.** The July sponsor exit slipped into August and the unplanned July FI bond deal offset smaller misses elsewhere.
- **September:** Lending −$0.5M and Debt Capital Markets −$0.5M, as a Large Corporate issuer postponed a bond to Q4.
- Q3 revenue was 3.2% lower than Q3 2025 and 6.4% lower than Q2 2026.

## Returns and cross-sell
- 18 Mid-Market clients hold Lending only and earn a trailing 12-month RAROC between 6% and 9%, against the 15% hurdle. Their loans use capital without the fee income that lifts returns. These clients are the priority cross-sell list for Q4: Transaction Banking operating accounts and Rates & FX hedges first, because both are low-capital and quick to implement.

## Coverage
- Coverage East 2 is the one team over capacity: 70 clients across 5 bankers, or 14 clients per banker against a target of 10. Its win rate has fallen every quarter, from 44% in Q4 2025 to 40%, 30% and 25% in Q3 2026. Other teams are at or below target.
- Recommendation: move about 15 Mid-Market relationships to Coverage East 1, which is well below its target of 8, and add one banker to Coverage East 2 in fiscal 2027.

## Pipeline
- Weighted pipeline was about $96.8M at quarter end across 111 open deals. The two Industrials mandates are the largest items in Execution.

## Controls
- The June Transaction Banking reconciliation break ($1.2M, general ledger above booked revenue) is still open and, under the SOP, is now on the CFO office watch list. Until the correction is posted, Transaction Banking revenue for June is understated by that amount.
- Data-quality checks quarantined a small number of source records this quarter (missing banker on coverage records, duplicate deal records and one negative fee); none changed reported revenue.

## Actions
1. Coverage East 1 to confirm November closing dates for the two Industrials mandates (owner: Head of Coverage East).
2. Coverage and product partners to agree account plans for the 18 lending-only Mid-Market clients by October 31 (owner: Head of Mid-Market Coverage).
3. COO to approve the Coverage East 2 rebalancing (owner: COO).
4. Finance to close the June Transaction Banking break before the October close (owner: Head of Finance).
