# Management Commentary: August 2026

Northlake Bank (fictional), synthetic. Monthly performance commentary for the Executive Committee, prepared by Business Management. Figures as reported at month-end close; all amounts in CAD.

## Headline
Revenue was $80.0M in August, $5.9M (6.9%) below plan. Two Industrials M&A mandates worth $7.5M in fees did not close; both are still in Execution and are now expected in mid-November. The sponsor exit that slipped from July closed this month and recovered part of the gap.

## Revenue vs plan
- **M&A Advisory, −$5.4M vs plan.** The two Industrials sell-side mandates ($4.5M and $3.0M expected fees, both planned for late August) slipped. In both cases the buyer asked for more time on regulatory approvals; neither client has cancelled the mandate. Under our definitions these are slipped deals: no revenue is booked until close, and they remain in the weighted pipeline at the Execution stage. Offsetting this, the sponsor exit planned for July closed and added $2.4M over August's sponsor plan.
- **Lending, −$0.5M vs plan.** Continued spread pressure on renewals.
- Debt and Equity Capital Markets, Rates & FX Hedging and Transaction Banking were close to plan.
- Industrials revenue was $12.7M against a $20.7M plan; the two mandates explain almost all of the gap.

## Pipeline and coverage
- Weighted pipeline is unchanged by the slips, because both mandates are still open at the same stage. The risk to Q4 is a further delay past the November target dates.
- Coverage East 2 remains above its capacity target; win-rate data for Q3 will be reported with the September close.

## Controls
- The June Transaction Banking reconciliation break ($1.2M, general ledger above booked revenue) is still open. Product Control has narrowed it to one fee batch and is waiting for the source-system extract. As a Severity 1 break under the SOP ($1M or more), it has been escalated to the Head of Finance. If it is still open at the September close, it goes on the CFO office watch list.

## Actions
1. Coverage East 1 to report weekly on regulatory milestones for both Industrials mandates (owner: Head of Coverage East).
2. Product Control to post the Transaction Banking correction once the extract is received (owner: Finance).
3. Business Management to re-phase the Q4 forecast for the two mandates without changing the approved plan (owner: COO).
